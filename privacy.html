Privacy Policy

streamclipper (TikTok app "clips27575") · Last updated October 20, 2026

streamclipper is a personal desktop tool. Its operator uses it to edit short clips from livestreams and publish them to the operator's own TikTok account. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up for it or use it.

What data the app handles

  • TikTok account access. When the operator connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token for the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. These tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer.
  • Basic profile information that TikTok returns, such as display name and avatar. It's used only to show which account is connected and which posting options TikTok allows for it.
  • Videos and captions that the operator reviews and approves before they're uploaded to their own TikTok account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from TikTok users other than the connected operator account.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not run on a server. All processing happens on the operator's computer.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the app, either by deleting the local token file or by revoking access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless right away.

Third-party services

Videos are uploaded directly to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs how TikTok handles content once it's posted.
